Privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026 (optional crash reports)

In short: there's no account and nothing to sign up for. Your lists and diaries live on your phone and the phones of the people you share them with. We (the developer) never receive them, and the app has no ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your lists, diary entries, their change history, the name you choose for yourself, and your settings are stored only on your phone. They are not backed up to any cloud service by the app.

What happens when you share a list or diary

Sharing gives the other person a QR code or link containing the list's secret key. Anyone with that code or link can see and change the list, so only share it with people you trust. Their phones and yours then keep the list in step:

  • Directly between phones where possible. To find each other, the phones briefly use free public "meeting point" servers (WebTorrent trackers) and address-lookup (STUN) servers. These see your phone's internet address and a random code for the list — never the list itself.
  • Through public relay servers (the Nostr network) when the phones can't connect directly, or when one of them is switched off. Everything sent this way is encrypted on your phone with the list's secret key, so the relays only see scrambled data, along with your internet address, the time and size of each message, and a random code for the list. Relayed messages are set to expire after 14 days.

The people you share with see the list's contents, the name you entered, and who changed what and when.

These servers are run by independent third parties, not by us; we don't control or receive their logs.

The share link page

Share links open a web page hosted on GitHub Pages that hands the link to the app. The list's details are in the part of the link after the "#", which web browsers never send to the server.

Permissions

  • Camera — only to scan a list's QR code. Pictures aren't saved or sent anywhere.
  • Photos — only the screenshot you choose, to read a QR code from it on your phone.
  • Notifications — to tell you when someone joins, leaves or changes a shared list. These are created on your phone.
  • Background sync — keeps the app connected so changes reach you while it's closed. You can turn it off in Settings.

What we collect

Nothing automatically. The app has no account, no analytics and no advertising, and it doesn't send any information to the developer by itself.

Crash reports, only if you choose. If the app closes unexpectedly, it notes the error on your phone. If you've turned on Settings › Offer to email crash reports, it offers to email a report to the developer; the email opens in your own email app so you can read it, and it's only sent if you send it. A report contains the app version, your phone's model and Android version, the time, and where in the app the error happened — nothing from your lists or calendar. The developer receives it as an ordinary email (with your email address) and uses it only to fix the problem. You can delete saved reports in Settings.

Deleting your data

Remove a list from your phone with ⋮ › Remove from this phone, or uninstall the app to remove everything. Copies on other members' phones are theirs to remove. Relayed messages expire on their own.

Children

The app isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
